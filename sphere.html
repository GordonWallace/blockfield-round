Blockfield Sphere

Prototype: a true sphere 655,360 blocks around. Blocks sit in rows of latitude, one block deep each; every row has as many columns as fit, so a block is within 3% of square everywhere except right by the poles. You move in real space, so a trip takes exactly as long as the distance on a real sphere. The HUD shows where you are as latitude and longitude, as a block (row, column, layer) and as x, y, z in space.

WASD move · Space jump · Ctrl sprint · F fly (Space/Shift up/down) · G fast fly · P go to a latitude and longitude · M mark here (the HUD then compares the distance walked with the straight line) · T random land · +/- view distance · left click break · right click place
